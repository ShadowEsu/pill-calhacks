# EDIT-BRIEF: 2:25 vertical pitch cut (1080×1920, 30 fps)
1. HOOK: say and show what Pill does in the first 3 s, with the caption on screen from frame 1 (most feeds autoplay muted). Devpost: "explain what your app does … in the first few seconds", https://help.devpost.com/article/84-video-making-best-practices · YouTube measures this as "Stayed to watch" vs swiped away, https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/12942217
2. SHOW THE PRODUCT WORKING: judges want the screencast, "without the gimmicks". Stickers and pill rain decorate the device and screen but never cover them; cut out the messy parts. https://help.devpost.com/article/84-video-making-best-practices
3. SAFE ZONE (organic Shorts): keep captions and stickers out of the top ~160–380 px, bottom ~320–380 px, right ~120–150 px and left ~60 px. Most robust: keep key content in x 60–930, y 380–1540. https://creamate.ai/en/tools/youtube-shorts-safe-zone · https://www.poster.ly/tools/safe-zone-checker
4. SAFE ZONE (strict, Google's vertical ad template): 288 top / 672 bottom / 48 left / 192 right, a safe box from (48,288) to (888,1248). Keep the most important sticker or verdict inside it. https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/13547298 (pixel values as measured by https://adchecklab.com/articles/youtube-shorts-safe-area-dimensions)
5. CAPTIONS: 2 lines max, ≤42 chars/line, ≤20 chars/s, each event on screen ≥0.83 s (5/6 s). https://partnerhelp.netflixstudios.com/hc/en-us/articles/217350977-English-USA-Timed-Text-Style-Guide · https://partnerhelp.netflixstudios.com/hc/en-us/articles/215758617-Timed-Text-Style-Guide-General-Requirements
6. KARAOKE: one highlight colour (yellow #FFE500) sweeping over white, black outline ≥4 px, 6–8 words/line max so each line reads in under 1 s. https://reelwords.ai/blog/how-to-add-captions-to-short-form-video
7. PACING: about one visual change per second in the first 3 s, then a cut, punch-in zoom (105–115%) or sticker every 2–4 s. Every cut needs a reason (action or text). https://mediagrowth.io/instagram/instagram-reels-views-delivery-pacing-captions-and-hook-structure-for-retention
8. MASTER LOUDNESS: final mix −14 to −16 LUFS integrated, true peak ≤ −1 dBTP. YouTube normalizes to −14 and turns louder files down. https://www.criticallisteninglab.com/en/learn/loudness/youtube · https://www.meterplugs.com/blog/2019/09/18/youtube-changes-loudness-reference-to-14-lufs.html
9. MUSIC BED: 18–25 LU under the voice, ducked a further 9–12 dB while someone is talking. https://zellahq.com/blog/music-ducking-explained · https://yiha.ai/blogs/background-music-while-talking-audio-mixing-and-ducking-guide · https://www.epidemicsound.com/blog/audio-mixing-for-video
10. SFX: whoosh and pop about −6 dB under speech ("felt, not heard"), only on cuts that matter, peak aligned to the cut or sticker landing. https://zellahq.com/blog/sound-design-at-cuts-whoosh-impact-riser · whoosh/transition default about −12 dB, https://videoeditingsfx.com/how-to-mix-sfx-levels
11. DARK PHONE FOOTAGE: brighten with gamma/mids, not lift. Keep blacks just off clip and soften shadows instead of lifting them (lifted blacks look flat and noisy). https://www.provideocoalition.com/solutions-to-resolve-part-3-unexpected-clipping-when-grading/ · https://noamkroll.com/how-to-color-grade-a-perfectly-cinematic-black-and-white-look/
12. END CARD: hold the final card ≥3 s (YouTube end screens run 5–20 s) and fade the music out over about 1.5 s under it. https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/6388789
13. UPLOAD EARLY: processing "could take anywhere from a few minutes to several hours". Upload as soon as the file is done, not at 08:59. https://help.devpost.com/article/84-video-making-best-practices
